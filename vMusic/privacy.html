Introduction

Welcome to vMusic! This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our music streaming application. vMusic is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about our data practices.

Important Note: vMusic is not affiliated with, funded, authorized, endorsed by, or in any way associated with YouTube, Google LLC or any of its affiliates and subsidiaries.

Information We Collect

1. Information You Provide

  • Playlist Data: Playlists you create, import, or synchronize
  • User Preferences: Theme settings, audio preferences, and customization options
  • Search History: Local search queries for songs, artists, and albums
  • Lyrics Data: Song lyrics you view or edit (stored locally)

2. Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: App usage statistics, crash reports, and performance metrics
  • Cached Content: Temporarily cached songs and metadata for offline playback
  • Network Information: IP address and network connection type for API requests

3. Information We Don't Collect

We DO NOT collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone)
  • Location data
  • Contacts or address book information
  • Payment information
  • Social media account information

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Service Provision: To provide music streaming, search, and playback functionality
  • Personalization: To customize your app experience with themes and preferences
  • Offline Access: To cache songs for offline playback
  • Performance Improvement: To analyze app performance and fix bugs
  • API Communication: To communicate with YouTube Music API for content retrieval
  • Playlist Management: To store and synchronize your playlists

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We take your privacy seriously and limit data sharing:

1. YouTube Music API

vMusic communicates with YouTube Music's API to retrieve music content. This includes:

  • Song metadata and streaming URLs
  • Search results for music discovery
  • Playlist information when you import playlists

2. No Third-Party Advertising

Good News: vMusic does not contain third-party advertising, and we do not sell your data to advertisers.

3. Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or government request, or to protect our rights, property, or safety.

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

  • Local Storage: Most data is stored locally on your device
  • Encrypted Communication: API communications use HTTPS encryption
  • Regular Updates: We regularly update our security practices
  • Minimal Data Collection: We only collect data necessary for app functionality

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information.

Your Privacy Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: View all data stored on your device through the app settings
  • Deletion: Clear cache, delete playlists, and reset preferences
  • Export: Export your playlists and settings
  • Opt-out: Disable crash reporting and analytics in settings
  • Uninstall: Completely remove the app and all associated data

Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide the vMusic service. You can delete your data at any time through the app settings or by uninstalling the application.

Third-Party Services

vMusic integrates with the following third-party services:

YouTube Music API

  • Purpose: Music content retrieval and streaming
  • Data shared: Search queries, content requests
  • Privacy policy: Subject to YouTube's terms of service

Open Source Libraries

vMusic uses open-source libraries that may have their own privacy policies. These libraries are used for:

  • Audio playback and processing
  • User interface components
  • Network communication

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Posting the new policy in the app
  • Updating the effective date
  • Sending in-app notifications for significant changes
