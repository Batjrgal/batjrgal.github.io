2. Information We Collect

Non‑Personal Information (optional): technical diagnostics such as extension version, runtime errors, and generic usage metrics.

Local Storage Data: preferences and settings saved using the Chrome Storage API to operate core features on facebook.com.

We do not collect personal identifiers (e.g., name, email) and we never access your Facebook credentials or the contents of your private messages.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide and maintain core functionality of the Extension on Facebook pages.
  • To remember your preferences locally for a better user experience.
  • To diagnose and improve performance and reliability.

We do not sell or rent any information to third parties.

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Data is stored locally in your browser via Chrome Storage API; no cloud transfer is performed by default.
  • Retention: local data persists until you clear it or uninstall the Extension.
  • Security: reasonable technical and organizational measures are applied; however, no method is 100% secure.

5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

  • We do not share personal data with third parties.
  • If third‑party services (e.g., crash reporting) are integrated in the future, we will update this policy and provide notice as appropriate.
  • Legal: we may disclose information if required by applicable law, regulation, or legal process.

6. Your Rights & Choices

  • Uninstall or disable the Extension at any time from your browser’s Extensions page.
  • Clear data saved by the Extension via your browser settings.
  • Contact us to request additional information about data handling.

7. Children’s Privacy

The Extension is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where applicable). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
